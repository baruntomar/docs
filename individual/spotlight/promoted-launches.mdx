Unlike [Free Launches](/individual/spotlight/free-launches), Promoted Launches do not require the **"Featured on desifounder"** badge or the requirement to like and comment on 5 other projects.

All Promoted Launch options are one-time purchases. There are no monthly or recurring subscriptions.

## Available Promoted Launch Options

### \$29 Instant Launch

A simple option for getting your project live immediately with extended promotional visibility.

Includes:

- Instant launch
- Permanent do-follow link
- 7 days pinned in the Promo section
- No badge required
- No mandatory requirement to like and comment on other projects, though it is recommended

_This option is suitable for projects that want immediate visibility and a permanent do-follow link without a longer promotional period._

### \$99 Featured Launch (Recommended)

A longer promotional placement for projects looking for extended visibility.

Includes:

- Instant launch
- Permanent do-follow link
- 30 days pinned in the Promo section
- No badge required
- No mandatory requirement to like and comment on other projects, though it is recommended

_This option gives your project a longer presence in the dedicated Promo section._

### \$129 Bulk Launch

Designed for creators or businesses that expect to launch multiple projects over a period of time.

Includes:

- Unlimited launches for 30 days
- Launches go live on Mondays
- No-follow links
- No badge required
- No mandatory requirement to like and comment on other projects, though it is recommended

_The 30-day period begins from the date of purchase. All launches under this option are subject to Spotlight's submission and content guidelines._

### \$149 Featured Launch + SEO (Best Value)

Our most comprehensive promotional option, combining extended Spotlight visibility with an additional SEO article.

Includes:

- Instant launch
- Permanent do-follow link
- 30 days pinned in the Promo section
- 1 dedicated SEO article delivered within 7 days
- No badge required
- No mandatory requirement to like and comment on other projects, though it is recommended

_The dedicated SEO article is created based on the information and materials provided by the project owner and is intended to provide additional editorial and search visibility._

## Do I Need to Add the desifounder Badge?

No.

The Featured on desifounder badge is mandatory for [Free Launches](/individual/spotlight/free-launches), but it is not required for Promoted Launches.

You can purchase and use a Promoted Launch without adding the badge to your website.

## What Does "Permanent Do-Follow Link" Mean?

Where a plan includes a permanent do-follow link, the project receives a do-follow link from its Spotlight listing to the destination URL provided by the project owner.

The link is intended to remain available as long as the applicable project listing remains on desifounder and is not removed under our policies or otherwise becomes unavailable.

_Desifounder does not guarantee any particular search engine ranking, traffic, domain authority, SEO performance, or other search-related outcome from a do-follow link._

## What Does "Pinned in the Promo Section" Mean?

Projects included in a Promoted Launch plan may be displayed in the dedicated Promo section for the duration specified by the selected plan.

- **\$29 Instant Launch:** 7 days
- **\$99 Featured Launch:** 30 days
- **\$149 Featured Launch + SEO:** 30 days

_The promotional period does not automatically renew._

## How Does Bulk Launch Work?

The \$129 Bulk Launch option allows unlimited project launches for 30 days.

Projects submitted under Bulk Launch are published on the applicable Monday launch cycle rather than being launched instantly.

Bulk Launch does not require a verified badge or mandatory liking and commenting weekly on 5 separate projects, thus making the submission time and process faster. This option is more applicable to creators or directories who do multiple-launches per week on behalf of the founders or products. 

Each project must still comply with the [Spotlight Rules & Guidelines](/individual/spotlight/rules-guidelines) and may be reviewed before publication.

_The Bulk Launch option does not include do-follow links. Links associated with these launches are no-follow (unless they get to Top 3 of the week; then they get a do-follow link)._

## Approval & Review

Payment does not guarantee publication.

All Promoted Launches remain subject to review and must comply with:

- [Spotlight Rules & Guidelines](/individual/spotlight/rules-guidelines)
- [Content Policies](/legal/content-policies)
- [Advertising & Sponsorship Policy](/legal/advertising-and-sponsorship-policy)
- Applicable laws and regulations

Desifounder may reject a submission that violates our policies or presents legal, security, reputational, or other concerns.

If a paid submission is rejected before publication, it may be eligible for a refund in accordance with our [**Refund & Cancellation Policy**](/legal/refund-and-cancellation-policy).

## One-Time Purchase

All Promoted Launch options are one-time purchases.

There are no automatic renewals or recurring charges.

Once the applicable promotional period ends, the promotional benefits associated with that purchase expire. If you want to continue promotional placement, you may purchase another available option.
