## **1. What is Spotlight?**

[Spotlight](https://desifounder.com/spotlight/?ref=help) is desifounder’s launch platform where founders, startups, and creators can showcase their products, get discovered by the community, and receive feedback and support.

Projects can be submitted for free or launched through one of our paid options for instant publishing and additional visibility.

## **2. Who can launch on Spotlight?**

Anyone with a genuine startup, product, digital product, app, tool, service, or project can submit to Spotlight.

You can launch your own project or a project you are part of, as long as it meets our submission guidelines.

## **3. Is it free to launch on Spotlight?**

Yes. You can submit and launch your project on Spotlight for free.

Free launches:

- Go live on Monday as part of the weekly Spotlight.
- Include a nofollow link to your project.
- Require the Featured on desifounder badge to be added to your website.
- Require you to like and comment on 5 different live projects before submitting your own.

The top 3 free launches of each week receive a dofollow link.

If you want to launch instantly or receive additional promotional visibility, you can choose one of our paid launch options.

## **4. What do I need to do before submitting a free launch?**

Before submitting a free launch, you need to contribute to the Spotlight community.

You must:

- Like 5 different live projects.
- Leave a helpful comment on 5 different live projects.
- Complete both requirements before submitting your own project.
- Add a “Featured on desifounder” badge to your website by embedding the code.

These requirements reset weekly based on when you last completed them. For example, if you complete your 5 likes and 5 comments on a Friday, your progress will reset the following Friday, and you'll need to complete the requirements again before your next free launch.

Comments should be genuine, thoughtful, and relevant to the project. Generic comments such as "Nice!" or "Looks cool!" may not count.

This helps make Spotlight a community where founders support other founders rather than simply promoting their own projects.

## **5. What is the "Featured on desifounder" badge?**

Free launches require the **Featured on desifounder** badge to be displayed on the project's website.

After submitting your project, you'll receive an embed code containing the badge image and a dofollow link to desifounder.

Simply add the provided code to your website. We'll verify the badge before approving your free launch.

The badge is not required for any paid launch option.

## **6. When can I launch on Spotlight?**

Free launches are scheduled to go live on Monday (00:00 IST) as part of the weekly Spotlight.

Paid launches can go live instantly on any day of the week.

Paid launches that go live during an ongoing Spotlight week are included in that week's Spotlight while also receiving the promotional benefits included with the selected plan.

## **8. Are Spotlight's paid options subscriptions?**

No.

All Spotlight paid options are one-time purchases and do not automatically renew.

Once the applicable promotional or launch period expires, you can purchase another option whenever you need it.

Your published Spotlight project remains available as a permanent listing, subject to our policies.

## **13. How does the weekly Spotlight work?**

Spotlight follows a Monday–Sunday weekly cycle.

Free launches are scheduled to go live on Monday (00:00 IST) and participate in that week's Spotlight.

Paid launches can go live instantly at any time during the week. Once launched, they are also included in the current week's Spotlight and remain available as a permanent listing.

At the end of each week, the weekly Spotlight rankings are finalized and a new Spotlight week begins.

## **14. How are the top 3 free launches selected?**

Free launches participate in the weekly Spotlight and compete based on community engagement and voting.

At the end of each Spotlight week, the top 3 free launches receive a dofollow link.

Other free launches retain their standard nofollow link.

Paid launches already receive a dofollow link as part of their applicable paid plan.

## **15. How long will my project stay on Spotlight?**

Your approved project becomes a permanent Spotlight listing.

The promotional period included with a paid launch only determines how long your project receives additional promotional placement.

For example:

- \$29 → 7 days of Promo placement
- \$99 → 30 days of Promo placement
- \$149 → 30 days of Promo placement + SEO article
- \$129 → 30 days of bulk-launch access

Once the applicable period ends, your project does not disappear from Spotlight.

## **16. What happens when my paid promotional period ends?**

Your project remains live as a permanent Spotlight listing.

Only the promotional benefits associated with your purchase expire.

For example, after your 7-day Promo placement under the \$29 option ends, your project will remain available on Spotlight but will no longer be pinned in the Promo section.

You can purchase another promotional option if you want additional visibility.

## **17. How does the approval process work?**

After submitting your project, our team will review it to make sure it meets our submission guidelines.

For free launches, we'll also verify that:

- You've completed the required community participation.
- The Featured on desifounder badge has been correctly added to your website.

Once your project is approved, you'll receive a confirmation email with your launch details.

Paid launches may be published instantly after payment and submission requirements are completed, subject to our submission guidelines and moderation.

## **18. Can I edit my submission after applying?**

You may be able to update certain details before your project goes live.

Once a project has been published, changes may require review to ensure the information remains accurate and appropriate for Spotlight.

## **19. Will I be notified when my project is approved?**

Yes.

Once your project has been approved, we'll send you a confirmation email with the relevant launch details.

For free launches, the email will also confirm when your project is scheduled to go live.

## **20. Can I launch more than one project?**

Yes.

You can submit multiple projects, provided each project meets our submission requirements.

If you're planning to launch several projects within a short period, the \$129 Bulk Launch option allows you to submit unlimited projects for 30 days, with launches going live on Mondays.

## **21. Can I launch instantly without adding the desifounder badge?**

Yes.

The Featured on desifounder badge is required only for free launches.

_The \$29, \$99, \$129, and \$149 paid options do not require the badge._

## **22. What kind of startups or projects are allowed on Spotlight?**

Spotlight is primarily for startups, products, apps, SaaS, digital products, tools, services, and other projects built by founders and creators.

Projects should provide genuine value and have enough information for the community to understand what you're building.

We may reject or remove submissions that are misleading, spammy, fraudulent, inappropriate, or otherwise don't fit the purpose of Spotlight.<br />
