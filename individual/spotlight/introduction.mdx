## What is Spotlight by desifounder?

[**Spotlight**](https://desifounder.com/spotlight/?ref=help) is desifounder’s launchpad for showcasing projects, products, and ideas created by founders, creators, and builders.

It gives you a dedicated space to share what you’re building, introduce your product to the desifounder community, and get your project discovered by potential users, customers, collaborators, and other members of the startup ecosystem.

## What can you do on Spotlight?

With Spotlight, you can:

- Launch your product, startup, or project.
- Share what you’re building with the community.
- Get feedback and early reactions from other members.
- Increase visibility and awareness for your project.
- Discover and connect with other founders and creators.

## Why launch on Spotlight?

Spotlight provides an opportunity to showcase your work while being part of a community of founders, creators, and builders.

By launching your project, you can:

- **Get discovered**: Put your project in front of the desifounder community.
- **Build awareness**: Create visibility for your product or project.
- **Get feedback**: Learn what others think about what you’re building.
- **Create connections**: Potentially connect with users, customers, collaborators, or other founders.
- **Gain recognition**: Eligible projects may receive additional exposure or recognition through desifounder.

## Free and Promoted Launches

Spotlight offers both **Free Launches** and **Promoted Launches**.

Free Launches allow eligible projects to be submitted to Spotlight without purchasing a promotional placement. Free launches are subject to the applicable submission requirements and availability.

Promoted Launches are paid promotional options that provide additional visibility and promotional benefits. The available options, pricing, and included benefits are described on the Promoted Launches page.

_Purchasing a Promoted Launch does not guarantee approval, engagement, clicks, signups, sales, funding, partnerships, or any other specific outcome._

## Recognition

desifounder may provide additional recognition or exposure to selected Spotlight projects through channels such as our newsletter, dedicated blogs, social media, or other community channels.

Where applicable, selected projects may also receive a special Promoted badge.

Recognition or featuring by desifounder does not constitute an endorsement, certification, investment recommendation, or guarantee of the project's quality or success.

## Important

Spotlight is a platform for discovering and showcasing projects. desifounder does not independently verify or guarantee the accuracy, quality, legality, performance, or claims made by every project featured on Spotlight.

Creators are responsible for the information and materials they submit, including ensuring that they have the necessary rights to use their content.

Users should conduct their own research before making any purchase, investment, partnership, or other decision based on information found through Spotlight.
