Launching your project on Spotlight is simple. You can submit your project as a **Free Launch** or choose a **Promoted Launch** if you want additional visibility and promotional benefits.

Follow these steps to get started:

## 1. Go to the Submission Page

Visit the [**Spotlight Submission Page**](https://desifounder.com/create/project) to start your project submission.

## 2. Add Your Project Details

Provide the required information about your project, including:

- **Project Name**: The name of your product, project, startup, or company.
- **One-liner**: A short statement that explains what your project does.
- **Description**: Tell the community what you are building, the problem it solves, and who it is for.
- **Category**: Select the category that best represents your project.
- **Project Link**: Add your official website or product link.

_Make sure the information you provide is accurate and clearly represents your project._

## 3. Add Your Project Visuals

Add the required images or other visual materials for your project.

We recommend using clear, high-quality visuals that help visitors quickly understand what you are building.

Only upload images, logos, videos, or other materials that you own or have permission to use.

## 4. Select Your Launch Option

Depending on the available options, you can choose between:

**Free Launch**: Submit your project without purchasing a promotional placement, subject to the applicable limits and availability. Launches go live every Monday at 00:00 IST.

**Promoted Launch**: Choose a paid promotional option if you want an instant launch and additional visibility plus benefits. The available options and pricing are shown during the submission process.

_You can review the details of each option before completing your submission._

## 5. Review Your Submission

Before submitting, check that:

- Your project information is accurate.
- Your links work correctly.
- Your images and other materials are suitable for publication.
- Your project follows the [**Spotlight Rules & Guidelines**](/individual/spotlight/rules-guidelines).
- You have the necessary rights to all content included in your submission.

## 6. Submit Your Project

Once everything is ready, click **Submit** to send your project to Spotlight.

All submissions may be reviewed by desifounder before publication. We reserve the right to approve, reject, request changes to, or remove a submission in accordance with our policies.

## 7. Your Project Goes Live

Once approved, your project will be published on Spotlight according to the launch option you selected.

Free and Promoted Launches may have different publication timing, visibility, placement, and promotional benefits. Please refer to the relevant Spotlight pages for details.

### A few tips before launching

- Keep your one-liner short and easy to understand.
- Clearly explain what makes your project useful or different.
- Use high-quality project visuals.
- Make sure your website or product link is working.
- Avoid exaggerated or misleading claims.
- Choose the most relevant category for your project.
