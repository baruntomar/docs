_<sub>Effective Date: October 05, 2026 • Last Updated: October 05, 2026</sub>_

It is designed for founders, creators, and builders who want to introduce their project to the desifounder community, discover other projects, and participate in the community before launching their own.

## What is included?

A Free Launch includes:

- A dedicated project listing on Spotlight.
- Visibility on the Spotlight feed, subject to applicable availability and placement.
- A link to your project or website.
- Your project being displayed on your desifounder profile.
- A "Featured on desifounder" badge for your website or project.
- An embed code that you can add to your website to display the badge and link back to your Spotlight project.
- An opportunity to be discovered and engaged with by the desifounder community.

## Community Participation Requirement

Before you can publish a Free Launch, you must participate in the Spotlight community by liking and commenting on at least 5 other Spotlight projects.

This requirement is intended to keep Spotlight community-driven and encourage creators to discover, support, and engage with other projects rather than using Spotlight only for self-promotion.

Comments should be relevant and meaningful. Desifounder may disregard or remove activity that appears to be spam, automated, or created solely to meet the participation requirement.

Once you have completed the required participation, you can proceed with your Free Launch submission.

## How to Launch for Free

1. Go to the [**Spotlight submission page**](https://desifounder.com/create/project).
2. Complete the required community participation by liking and commenting on 5 other Spotlight projects.
3. Enter your project information and upload the required materials.
4. Select the _Free Launch_ option.
5. Review your submission and submit it.
6. Your submission may be reviewed by the desifounder team.
7. Once approved, your project will be published according to the applicable Free Launch availability and placement rules.

## "Featured on desifounder" Badge

Every eligible Free Launch includes a **"Featured on desifounder"** badge that you can add to your website or project.

The badge includes a link to your Spotlight project and can help visitors discover your project on desifounder.

After your project is published, you can access the provided embed code and add it to your website.

Adding the badge to your website is mandatory for your Free Launch.

_Please do not modify the badge or use the embed code to misrepresent your relationship with desifounder._

## Free Launch Availability

To maintain a balanced and sustainable Spotlight feed, the number of Free Launches may be limited.

Desifounder may set or change the number of Free Launch slots available during a particular period based on platform capacity, demand, or other operational considerations.

The availability shown during the submission process will apply at the time you submit your project.

## Free Launch vs. Promoted Launch

A Free Launch provides a standard way to showcase your project on Spotlight.

If you want additional visibility or promotional benefits, you may choose one of the available Promoted Launch options.

Promoted Launches may include benefits such as enhanced placement, extended visibility, additional links, or other promotional features depending on the option selected.

See **Spotlight: Promoted Launches** for current options and pricing.

## What a Free Launch does not guarantee

A Free Launch does not guarantee:

- A specific number of views or impressions.
- Clicks or website visits.
- Likes, comments, votes, or other engagement.
- Customers, users, leads, or sales.
- Funding or investment.
- Partnerships or collaborations.
- Any specific business or commercial outcome.

The visibility and engagement of a project may vary depending on community activity, the quality of the submission, timing, and other factors.

## Can I Promote My Free Launch?

If you want additional visibility after launching for free, you may be able to purchase an available Promoted Launch option, subject to the options and availability offered by desifounder at that time.

Purchasing a Promoted Launch does not guarantee any particular level of engagement or business results.
