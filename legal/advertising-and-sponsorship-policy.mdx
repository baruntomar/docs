This Advertising & Sponsorship Policy explains the rules that apply to advertising, sponsorships, paid placements, featured listings, promotional campaigns, sponsored content, and other paid promotional activities on desifounder (“desifounder”, “we”, “our”, or “us”).

This policy applies to advertising across the Platform, including where applicable:

- Spotlight Promoted Launches;
- featured or promoted listings;
- sponsored placements;
- newsletter placements;
- sponsored articles or content;
- promotional posts;
- social media promotions;
- event or service promotions; and
- other advertising products or formats offered by desifounder.

By purchasing, submitting, or participating in a paid promotional campaign, you agree to this policy, our [Terms & Conditions](/legal/terms-and-conditions), [Content Policies](/legal/content-policies), [Refund & Cancellation Policy](/legal/refund-and-cancellation-policy), and applicable laws.

## 1. Advertising Eligibility

We generally accept advertising that is relevant to the interests of the desifounder community, including:

- startups and entrepreneurship;
- software and technology products;
- business and professional services;
- developer and productivity tools;
- educational products and resources;
- startup events and conferences;
- fundraising and investment-related opportunities;
- marketing, finance, and operational tools;
- products and services for founders and businesses; and
- other legitimate products or services that are reasonably relevant to our audience.

Acceptance of an advertisement is at the discretion of desifounder.

We may reject an advertisement even if it does not fall within a specific prohibited category where we reasonably believe the advertisement could create legal, safety, reputational, community, or user-experience concerns.

## 2. Prohibited Advertisements

We do not accept advertisements that violate applicable law, our policies, or the standards described below.

### a) Illegal Activities

Advertisements must not promote:

- illegal products or services;
- fraudulent schemes;
- counterfeit goods;
- unlawful financial activities;
- unauthorized financial services;
- criminal activity; or
- services intended to facilitate illegal activity.

### b) Fraudulent or Deceptive Advertising

Advertisements must not contain intentionally false, misleading, or deceptive claims.

This includes:

- fake testimonials or reviews;
- fabricated customer numbers;
- fabricated funding or investment claims;
- manipulated statistics presented as genuine;
- fake partnerships;
- misleading pricing;
- false scarcity claims;
- unrealistic guarantees;
- deceptive before-and-after claims;
- misleading claims about product capabilities; or
- claims that materially misrepresent the advertiser, product, service, or opportunity.

Advertisers are responsible for being able to reasonably substantiate material claims made in their advertisements.

### c) Harmful or Abusive Content

Advertisements must not contain:

- hate speech;
- targeted harassment;
- threats;
- encouragement of violence;
- discriminatory abuse;
- sexual exploitation;
- pornography or sexually explicit material; or
- content that promotes serious harm.

### d) Intellectual Property Violations

Advertisements must not use another person's or organization's:

- trademark;
- logo;
- copyrighted material;
- images;
- videos;
- software;
- brand identity; or
- other proprietary material

without appropriate authorization or fair use policy.

_Advertisers are responsible for obtaining all necessary rights and permissions._

### e) Privacy Violations

Advertisements must not unlawfully collect, use, disclose, or request personal information.

Landing pages and promotional campaigns must comply with applicable privacy and data-protection requirements.

### f) Malware, Phishing, and Security Risks

We do not permit advertisements that lead to or promote:

- malware;
- ransomware;
- phishing pages;
- credential theft;
- malicious downloads;
- harmful scripts;
- deceptive browser extensions;
- malicious software; or
- other significant security threats.

_We may restrict or remove an advertisement if its destination appears unsafe or compromised._

## 3. Restricted Categories

Certain categories may be rejected or restricted even where the underlying product or service may be lawful.

These may include:

- gambling or betting;
- tobacco and nicotine products;
- recreational drugs;
- weapons and firearms;
- high-risk financial products;
- high-risk cryptocurrency or token projects;
- multi-level marketing schemes;
- get-rich-quick schemes;
- political campaigns or political advertising;
- adult services;
- products or services directed primarily at exploiting vulnerable people; and
- other categories that present significant legal, safety, reputational, or community risks.

The fact that a product or service is legal in a particular jurisdiction does not require desifounder to accept advertising for it.

_We may update or expand restricted categories as the Platform evolves._

## 4. Financial, Investment, and Funding Advertising

Advertisements relating to investments, fundraising, financial products, lending, trading, cryptocurrency, or similar activities may receive additional scrutiny.

Advertisers are responsible for ensuring that:

- the offering is lawful;
- all required licenses or registrations are in place;
- required disclosures are provided;
- claims about returns or performance are accurate and appropriately qualified;
- risks are not intentionally hidden or understated; and
- the advertisement complies with applicable financial and securities laws.

desifounder does not independently verify every financial or investment claim.

Publication of a financial or funding-related advertisement does not mean that desifounder recommends, endorses, verifies, or guarantees the advertised opportunity.

_Users should conduct their own due diligence before making any financial decision._

- the landing page changes materially;

- we receive a valid legal request;
- the advertisement creates a significant risk to users or the Platform; or
- circumstances arise that make continued publication inappropriate.

_Where appropriate, we may contact the advertiser before taking action, but we may act immediately where necessary._
