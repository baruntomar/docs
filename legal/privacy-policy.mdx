Welcome to [**desifounder.com**](http://desifounder.com/?ref=help) (“desifounder”, “we”, “our”, or “us”).

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when we share it, and the choices you have when using desifounder.

This Privacy Policy applies to our website, user profiles, Community Feed, Spotlight, Events, Fund, Services, Referral Program, and other features or services provided through desifounder (collectively, the “Platform”).

By creating an account or using the Platform, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required by applicable law, we will request that consent through an appropriate notice or consent mechanism.

## 1. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use desifounder.

### a) Account and Profile Information

When you create or update an account, we may collect:

- First and last name
- Username
- Email address
- Authentication information
- Profile picture
- Phone number, if provided (private)
- Date of birth, if provided (private)
- Gender, if provided (private)
- Location, if provided
- Website
- Industry
- Bio
- Social media links
- Other information you choose to add to your profile

Some information may be designated as private within your account and may not be publicly displayed.

If you sign in using a third-party authentication provider, such as Google, we may receive information provided to us through that authentication process, subject to the provider's own policies.

### b) User-Submitted Content

We collect information and content that you voluntarily submit to the Platform, including:

- Spotlight projects and launches
- Community Feed posts
- Comments and replies
- Likes and other interactions
- Event information
- Service listings
- Funding-related submissions
- Referral activity
- Profile information
- Images, links, descriptions, and other materials you upload
- Information included in communications with us

Some of this information may be publicly visible depending on the feature and your available privacy settings.

### c) Activity and Platform Usage

We may collect information about how you interact with the Platform, such as:

- Pages and sections you visit
- Features you use
- Posts or projects you interact with
- Search or discovery activity
- Likes, comments, follows, reports, and similar actions
- Account and session activity
- Device and browser information
- IP address
- Approximate technical or geographic information derived from your connection
- Error logs and diagnostic information

We use this information to operate, secure, maintain, and improve the Platform.

### d) Payment and Transaction Information

If you purchase a paid feature or service, we may collect information relating to the transaction, such as:

- The product or service purchased
- Purchase date and amount
- Transaction or payment reference
- Payment status
- Billing information where required

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers.

We generally do not need to store your complete payment-card details when those details are processed directly by a payment provider. Payment providers may process your information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

### e) Communications

If you contact us, we may retain the information you provide, including your email address, message, attachments, and other information necessary to respond to your request.

We may also retain communications relating to reports, complaints, disputes, account issues, legal requests, or other Platform-related matters.

### f) Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to:

- keep you signed in;
- remember preferences;
- maintain security;
- understand how the Platform is used;
- improve functionality and performance; and
- measure traffic and usage.

You can control certain cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect the functionality of parts of the Platform.

Depending on the feature, this may include:

- your name and username;
- profile picture;
- bio;
- website and social links;
- projects;
- Spotlight submissions;
- Community Feed posts and comments;
- service listings;

If you submit a project to Spotlight, we may use the information and materials you provide to publish and promote that project according to the Spotlight option you select.

This may include displaying your project:
