## 2. General Refund Policy

Because our paid services are digital services and promotional placements, purchases are generally non-refundable once the purchased service has been successfully activated, published, or delivered.

However, refunds may be available in the specific circumstances described below.

Nothing in this policy limits any refund or consumer right that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.

## 3. Paid Spotlight Submission Rejected Before Publication

A paid Spotlight submission may be reviewed before publication.

If desifounder rejects a paid Spotlight submission before it is published or activated, the amount paid for that specific submission will be refunded.

A rejection may occur where a submission:

- violates our [Terms & Conditions](/legal/terms-and-conditions);
- violates our [Content Policies](/legal/content-policies) or [Spotlight Rules](/individual/spotlight/rules-guidelines);
- contains misleading or fraudulent information;
- infringes third-party rights;
- promotes prohibited content or activities;
- presents a security, legal, or reputational risk; or
- otherwise does not meet the requirements for publication.

Payment does not guarantee approval.

_If a paid submission is rejected before publication, the refund will normally be issued to the original payment method, subject to the payment provider's processing procedures._

## 4. Published or Activated Services

Once a paid Spotlight launch has been approved and published or otherwise activated, the purchase is generally considered fulfilled and is not refundable.

This includes situations where the customer:

- changes their mind;
- no longer wants the launch;
- changes the project after purchase;
- decides not to promote the project;
- receives fewer views or clicks than expected;
- does not receive the expected engagement;
- does not receive customers or signups; or
- does not achieve the expected business or SEO results.

## 5. No Refund for Performance or Business Results

Purchasing a promotional service does not guarantee any particular result.

Refunds will not be provided solely because a customer does not achieve a desired level of:

- impressions;
- views;
- clicks;
- likes;
- comments;
- votes;
- followers;
- website traffic;
- signups;
- customers;
- sales;
- revenue;
- leads;
- funding;
- partnerships;
- search-engine rankings;
- SEO traffic; or
- other business outcomes.

_These are performance outcomes and are outside our control._

## 6. Cancellation Before Publication

If you want to cancel a paid Spotlight submission before it has been published, contact us as soon as possible.

Cancellation does not automatically guarantee a refund.

If the submission has already entered review, been approved, been scheduled, or work has already begun on the purchased service, the applicable refund provisions of this policy will apply.

Where a paid submission is rejected by desifounder before publication, the refund provisions in Section 3 will apply.

## 8. Promotional Placement Periods

Where a plan includes a defined promotional period, such as 7 or 30 days, the service is considered fulfilled when the promotional placement is activated and made available for the applicable period, subject to reasonable interruptions caused by maintenance, technical problems, or circumstances outside our reasonable control.

A temporary technical interruption does not automatically create a right to a refund.

Where a material service interruption is caused by desifounder and significantly affects the purchased promotional period, we may provide an extension, replacement placement, credit, or other reasonable remedy.

The permanent nature of the link does not guarantee:

- search-engine indexing;

A link may be removed where reasonably necessary due to:

- violation of our policies;

- a legal requirement; or

_Removal for such reasons does not automatically create a refund entitlement._
